Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Brass Pulse KFK does not collect, sell, or share personal data. The app does not track users and does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

App Data

Brass Pulse KFK stores app settings and practice data on your device, such as saved songs, tempo, time signatures, loop ranges, count-in settings, and rhythm groupings. This information is used only to make the app work and remains local to your device.

Tracking

Brass Pulse KFK does not track you across apps or websites.
