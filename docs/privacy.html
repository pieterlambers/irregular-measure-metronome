App & Manual Privacy Policy

Brass Pulse KFK Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Brass Pulse KFK does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app may save settings and metronome preferences locally on your device so the app can restore your setup the next time you open it. This information stays on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Network Access

Brass Pulse KFK does not require an account and does not send app usage data to a server.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the GitHub repository for this app.
